The Label

About

An independent record label for music that means something. Founded on the belief that the best art cuts through the noise.

The Story

Scissors Beats Rock started in 2023 with a simple premise: there's no shortage of incredible indie music being made, but there is a shortage of labels willing to take the long view. We're not interested in algorithms or viral moments. We're interested in records that sound better the tenth time you play them.

The name comes from the childhood game — a reminder that the underdog wins more often than you'd think. Every band we sign is a bet that art made with conviction beats art made by committee. So far, we're winning.

The Philosophy

We believe in physical media. We believe in fair deals for artists. We believe that a record label's job is to remove obstacles so musicians can focus on making music. We handle the boring stuff — pressing, distribution, promotion — so our artists can focus on the things that matter.

"Every record we put out should sound like it couldn't have been made by anyone else. That's the bar."

The Sound

Our catalog lives in the space between nostalgia and nowness. You'll hear echoes of Yo La Tengo, Pavement, Slowdive, Built to Spill, and American Football — but filtered through artists who grew up on those records and have something new to say. Guitar music isn't dead. It just moved to smaller rooms.

The Team

SBR is run by a small crew of music obsessives who've spent years in and around independent music — playing in bands, booking shows, running distros, and staying up too late arguing about Pixies B-sides. We're based on the East Coast but our artists span the country.
