February 18, 2026 Release

Worn Photographs Announce Debut LP & Spring Tour

After two years of quietly building a reputation through basement shows and word-of-mouth tape trades, Portland's Worn Photographs are finally releasing their debut full-length, Everything We Left Behind. The album drops March 14th on 180g vinyl, chrome cassette, and all digital platforms. A 12-date spring tour follows, hitting independent venues across the West Coast and Midwest.

We're heading to Austin. The full Scissors Beats Rock roster will perform at Hotel Vegas on March 8th as part of our first official SXSW showcase. Doors at 7pm, all ages. Weathervane Hearts Signs to Scissors Beats Rock

We're thrilled to welcome Weathervane Hearts to the SBR family. The Portland four-piece brings a folk-punk sensibility to the roster — banjos and distortion pedals in equal measure. Their debut album for the label is expected this fall. In the meantime, their cassette EP Turning Season is available now in the store.
